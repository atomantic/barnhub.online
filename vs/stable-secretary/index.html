Why pick BarnHub over Stable Secretary

Stable Secretary is a respected SaaS in the barn-management space, with tiers (Essentials, Performance, Professional, Go!) that bundle scheduling, billing, multi-user access, and a client portal. The trade-off is the same one every cloud SaaS asks: a monthly bill, a cloud account for everyone in your barn, and your boarder roster sitting on someone else's servers. BarnHub is the opposite trade — a native Apple app that your barn owns outright, with data in your own iCloud.

  • One-time download, no subscription. BarnHub is free on the App Store with an optional one-time Pro upgrade. No monthly bill, no annual renewal, no ramp from a "starter" tier into a "pro" tier as your barn grows.
  • Your data, your iCloud. Records sync via Apple CloudKit between your own devices. Nothing lives on a third-party server, so there's no vendor outage that can lock you out of your barn's records.
  • No accounts for anyone. Boarders don't need a login. You don't need a login. Apple ID is the identity. There's no "your boarder forgot their password" support burden.
  • Native on every Apple device. SwiftUI on iPhone, iPad, and Mac — same UI density everywhere — versus a web-first product whose mobile and desktop experiences are inevitably different.
  • Zero tracking, zero analytics. Apple's Data Not Collected label. BarnHub doesn't know what your boarders are named, doesn't run a recommendation engine, doesn't sell aggregated insights to feed manufacturers.
  • Works fully offline. The barn aisle has bad WiFi. BarnHub keeps working; cloud SaaS apps need a connection to load.

What Stable Secretary actually gives you

To be fair: there are real reasons to pick a SaaS like Stable Secretary, and they're worth naming. If any of these are core to how your barn operates, BarnHub may not be the right fit yet:

  • Boarder-facing client portal. Owners log in to see their horse, their balance, their next farrier visit. BarnHub does not have a logged-in client surface today.
  • Built-in Stripe payouts. Stable Secretary's Professional tier collects card payments inside the app. BarnHub records payments but doesn't process them.
  • Cross-platform team. Trainers and barn staff on Android phones can use Stable Secretary. BarnHub is iOS-only.
  • Multi-user staff with permissions. Stable Secretary lets you scope which staff member sees what. BarnHub today is one user / one Apple ID.

Frequently asked questions

Why use BarnHub instead of Stable Secretary?

Stable Secretary is a paid SaaS with monthly tiers and your barn's data hosted on their servers. BarnHub is a one-time-buy native app where your data lives in your own iCloud. If you don't need a customer-facing booking portal or built-in Stripe payouts, BarnHub does the day-to-day barn operations work without an ongoing subscription or a cloud account for your boarders.

Does BarnHub have a client portal like Stable Secretary?

Not today. BarnHub generates client-facing PDFs (invoices, boarding contracts) you can email, but it does not have a logged-in boarder portal. If your operation depends on boarders self-serving online, Stable Secretary or another SaaS will fit better.

Can BarnHub take payments through Stripe?

BarnHub is a ledger — it tracks income, expenses, and per-client payment history, but it does not process card payments. Most barns we hear from already have an existing Stripe, Square, Venmo, or check-based workflow with their boarders, and use BarnHub to record what was paid.

Will I lose data if I cancel a subscription?

BarnHub has no subscription to cancel. Your records live in your iCloud and on your devices. You can also export to CSV at any time. With a SaaS, your data lives on the vendor's servers and access ends when you stop paying.

Is there an Android version?

BarnHub is Apple-only (iPhone, iPad, Mac). Stable Secretary has Android, so if your team is mixed-platform, either run BarnHub on the iOS devices and export shared records, or pick a cross-platform tool.
